# Archetypes (12, own wording)
Pick a primary and a shadow (the one that shows when under stress or when the brand drifts). Explain why not the neighbour. Use only as a steering tool: the book states behaviours, not labels.

| Archetype | Core desire | Voice cue | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| Innocent | safety, simplicity | plain, warm | naive |
| Sage | understanding | calm, evidence-led | dry, aloof |
| Explorer | freedom, discovery | curious, first-hand | restless |
| Outlaw | disruption | blunt, defiant | angry |
| Magician | transformation | vivid, visionary | vague |
| Hero | mastery, courage | direct, driven | arrogant |
| Lover | intimacy | sensory, close | cloying |
| Jester | joy | playful, irreverent | flippant |
| Everyman | belonging | down to earth | bland |
| Caregiver | service | reassuring | smothering |
| Ruler | order, control | authoritative | cold |
| Creator | expression, craft | imaginative, process-aware | precious |

Method: score each archetype 0-3 on (a) the user's stated belief, (b) their own writing samples, (c) what the audience wants, (d) what competitors already own. Top two become primary/shadow. Write one example line in the brand's voice for each. Film-maker examples: a field documentarian who films with people not about them tends toward Explorer + Creator (shadow: Sage that lectures).
